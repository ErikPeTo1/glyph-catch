# Changelog

All notable changes to Glyph Catch since it was continued from [Equal Parts' original](https://github.com/equalparts/glyph-catch).

## 3.7.0

- Phone (4a) Pro support (beta): a Poké Ball lights up on the Glyph Matrix when a Pokémon is waiting. See who it is on Home or in the waiting Pokémon widget and throw a Poké Ball to catch it, with the catch playing on the Glyph Matrix. There's also a Poké Ball widget, and Professor Oak explains it all the first time.
- Super Repel: the clerk brings it at 150 species. While it's on, only Pokémon you don't have in your PC come close.
- Honey: a Bug Catcher gives it to you soon after the Sinnoh Pokédex. Spread it from your Bag and Pokémon with a sweet tooth, like Combee or Heracross, come for an hour. Munchlax is only found this way now, it no longer hatches from Eggs.
- Incense: Pokémon appear twice as fast for an hour. Win it in missions or find one now and then when you catch a Pokémon.
- Legendary Pokémon are never scared off by a repel.
- Home shows a banner while Honey or an Incense is working.
- Fixed the team looking full after releasing one of its Pokémon. Existing saves are fixed when you open the app.
- The Plain wallpaper keeps its frame on tall widgets.
- Updated to Nothing's Glyph Matrix SDK 2.0.
- 3 new tips.

## 3.6.1

- Fixed Pokémon looking like a negative on the Glyph Matrix when dimmed (the companion and the "Lower brightness" setting): highlights and shadows are back where they belong.

## 3.6.0

- Achievements: 15 extra PC Box wallpapers from Pokémon Emerald, each one unlocked by an achievement, like catching a Pikachu, hatching 10 Eggs or your first shiny. Locked ones show what it takes and how far you are.
- Companion: with no wild Pokémon waiting, the Glyph Matrix can show a Pokémon of your choice instead of the clock, a bit dimmer so it's never mistaken for a wild one. Pick it from its details.
- Each Pokémon's details show its shiny chain: the three steps, how far you are and, once you've caught one, its shiny.
- In the PC: sort by "Most repeated", the "Repeated" filter (different forms count separately) and "All but one" to release duplicates while keeping one of each.
- Legendary Pokémon are caught at level 70–80 (Arceus 80–90); gentle mythicals like Mew or Jirachi at 30–45.
- Tyrogue can evolve into Hitmonlee and Hitmonchan too, not just Hitmontop.
- 6 new tips.

## 3.5.0

- Two home-screen widgets: your **team**, up to six favorites picked from each Pokémon's details, and the **Pokémon waiting** on your Glyph Matrix. They adapt to any size and update as you play.
- Widget backgrounds: any color with RGB and opacity sliders, or one of the 16 PC Box wallpapers from Pokémon Emerald. The waiting Pokémon widget can also pick the box that fits whoever is at the front.
- Exp. Share: Professor Oak gives it to you once you reach Unova and asks whether you want it on. With it on, your whole team earns experience while your phone is locked, a little less than the Pokémon you're training. Switch it from the Bag.
- Settings are easier to find: a new Appearance section, icons, and summaries of your weather and bedtime.
- The Glyph Matrix could stop updating for a while when the system stopped sending its minute ticks. Glyph Catch now keeps its own clock as a backup.

## 3.4.0

- The more times you catch a species, the more likely its shiny becomes: twice as likely after 10 catches, three times after 25 and four times after 50. Released Pokémon still count, and each Pokémon's details show how far you are.
- Missions that reward Rare Candies now give 30 (daily) or 100 (weekly).
- The app checks for updates every time you open it.

## 3.3.0

- Electric-type Pokémon gather around your phone while it's charging, like Pikachu, Magnemite or Shinx. Joltik, which feeds on the tiniest static charge, gets a chance of its own.
- Spacing fix in the Pokégear.

## 3.2.0

- When using Rare Candies, you can see whether the Pokémon will evolve and into what (including whole evolution lines, random evolutions and the Everstone).
- Releasing a Pokémon gives you a Rare Candy.
- The Pokémon list can also be shown as a 3-column grid, like the Pokédex. Switching keeps your place.
- Shiny Pokémon keep their colors in the evolution notification.
- Eggs now use their sprite from the main games.
- Warnings and the release button use the app's red.

## 3.1.0

- The Fisherman gives you the fishing rods, a clerk the Repel, and a Pokémon Breeder explains how Eggs work and gives you your first one.
- Professors call you: a red dot on the phone icon means one of them has something for you on the Pokégear.
- New games almost always start with a starter Pokémon (with a small chance of a pseudo-legendary's first stage).
- Double-tap a tab to go back to the top of that section.
- Fixed: after an update, Pokémon could take a long time to appear.

## 3.0.0

**Professors and regions**
- Professor Oak welcomes you and gives you the Kanto Pokédex. New players start with Pokémon from Kanto only.
- Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh and Unova open when their professor gives you the regional Pokédex, at 50, 120, 200 and 300 Pokémon.
- The Pokégear: call the professors to hear how your regional Pokédex is going and a different rumor each time.
- With all four regional Pokédexes, Professor Oak gives you the Town Map to choose which regions' Pokémon appear.
- When a professor has something for you, the phone on the Home screen shows a red dot: call them on the Pokégear to collect it. Existing saves can collect everything they've already earned.

**Legendary Pokémon**
- Many legendary Pokémon need their key item to appear, like the Eon Ticket for Latias and Latios or the Meteorite for Deoxys. Their professor calls you with it when you hear the rumor.
- A released legendary Pokémon can appear again, one at a time, so you can hunt for its shiny.
- Complete the national Pokédex to get the Shiny Charm: shinies are three times as common (it can be switched off in the Bag).

**Eggs**
- Baby Pokémon only hatch from Eggs. You might find one when you catch a Pokémon, from the Johto Pokédex on.
- Carry up to three. They hatch after 2, 5 or 10 hours with the phone locked, and are four times as likely to be shiny.

**Items and sprites**
- Use several Rare Candies at once (hold + or − to go faster). Your training partner is listed first when an item works on it.
- When giving an evolution item, you can see whether you've already registered the evolution.
- Gen 2-5 sprites retuned for the Glyph Matrix, whose brightness isn't linear.
- On first launch, choose between color sprites and the black-and-white Glyph Matrix style.

## 2.0.0

**Content**
- 649 Pokémon (Gens 1-5) with current types and evolutions, including baby Pokémon.
- New evolution items: Shiny, Dusk, Dawn and Ice Stones. The Sun Stone can now be found too.
- You choose what Tyrogue and Clamperl evolve into. Wurmple and Burmy decide for themselves, like in the main games.
- Everstone toggle to stop a Pokémon from evolving when it levels up.
- Alternate forms: Castform (weather), Deerling/Sawsbuck (season), all 28 Unown, Burmy/Wormadam cloaks and Shellos/Gastrodon seas.

**Spawning**
- The streak only resets when you unlock your phone, so checking the time on the lock screen is fine.
- Faster spawns: one is guaranteed after 30 minutes without unlocking.
- Up to nine Pokémon can wait on the Glyph Matrix, and the rarest one is shown first.
- New Very Rare tier for pseudo-legendaries, starters from other regions and a few special Pokémon.
- Three fishing rods (Old, Good and Super), each with its own Pokémon.
- Every legendary and mythical Pokémon has its own conditions to appear. The Rumors page gives hints as your Pokédex grows.
- New events: Valentine's Day, Pokémon Day, New Year and San Juan (Midsummer), plus extra Halloween and thunderstorm Pokémon.
- Pokémon keep appearing quietly while you sleep, without animations.

**Shinies**
- 1 in 512 wild Pokémon is shiny. On the Glyph Matrix it shows as a negative image and flashes longer when it appears.
- Odds are doubled during the Halloween and Christmas weeks, and quadrupled on special days.
- Shiny colors in the app and a "Shinydex" in the Pokédex.

**Quality of life**
- Sort and mass-release Pokémon in the PC (favorites and your training partner are protected).
- Sort your Bag, and browse the Pokédex by region.
- Export and import your progress to a file.
- Statistics: time without unlocking each day, with a 7-day chart.
- Daily and weekly missions.
- Optional color sprites in the app (the Glyph Matrix stays monochrome).
- Available in English, Spanish, German, French, Italian and Portuguese.
